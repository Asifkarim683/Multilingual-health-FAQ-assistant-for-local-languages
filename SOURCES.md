# Health FAQ Assistant: Trusted Public Health Sources

This document lists all curated public health knowledge base sources used by the Multilingual Health FAQ Assistant.

All content is strictly sourced from verified public health authorities (WHO, MoHFW India, ICMR, NVBDCP, Odisha NHM) under public health educational and open publication licenses or terms of reuse. No proprietary or patient data is used.

## 2. Ingestion and Usage Terms

- **Informational and Educational Use Only**: Passages ingested from these documents are used strictly to ground FAQ retrieval.
- **No Diagnostic Modification**: Text is extracted verbatim or summarized with fidelity; medical facts are not altered.
- **Attribution**: Every retrieved answer must cite the corresponding `SRC-XX` ID, title, and link.
- **Licensing Compliance**: Sources prohibiting redistribution or commercial licensing are excluded.
